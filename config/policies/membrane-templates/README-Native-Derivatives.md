# DOPC and DPPC native-patch candidates

`DOPC-OpenMM86-63x63.pdb` and `DPPC-OpenMM86-63x63.pdb` are **validation candidates**, not enabled construction policies. They are deterministic derivatives of the separately installed OpenMM 8.6.0 application patches. Run `out/python/bin/python scripts/generate-native-defect-deletion-patches.py DOPC --check` and the same command with `DPPC` to reproduce and verify them without writing. `--write` regenerates the exact candidate after checking the pinned source and single-molecule reference.

All 128 source lipids were checked against their exact Lipid21 atom order, bonds and stereo descriptors. Only the named DOPC residues have a trans second alkene; only the named DPPC residues have inverted glycerol handedness. Known-correct source/template and deliberately inverted controls are in `test_native_patch_chemistry_controls.py`. A rigid correction of DOPC's downstream double-bond group made the two cis descriptors agree but moved 27 atoms per affected molecule by as much as 17.20 Å and introduced a 1.287 Å intermolecular heavy contact, below the existing 1.5 Å starting floor. DPPC's affected C2 hydrogens point along their ordinary tetrahedral directions relative to the three heavy branches; moving H alone would not correct the heavy arrangement. Deleting the one defective molecule on each leaflet is the bounded candidate that preserves every surviving molecule's source coordinates, bonds, atom identity and periodic cell without a new conformer-conditioning method.

The DOPC candidate retains a 62.104 × 66.555 × 76.388 Å cell, 63 DOP per leaflet and a 65.608 Å² geometric area per retained lipid; the source had 64 per leaflet and 64.583 Å². Mean phosphate-head separation changes from 39.729 to 39.748 Å. The removed positions are 6.27 and 5.44 Å from the nearest surviving phosphate. The DPPC candidate retains a 55.865 × 56.886 × 80.993 Å cell, 63 DPP per leaflet and 50.443 Å² per retained lipid; the source had 64 per leaflet and 49.655 Å². Mean phosphate-head separation changes from 43.015 to 43.069 Å. Its removed positions are 4.43 and 5.40 Å from the nearest surviving phosphate. These are source/derivative geometry observations, **not** evidence of pre-equilibration or acceptable final membrane organization. OpenMM's `Modeller.addMembrane` describes a pre-equilibrated input patch; the two vacancies in each derivative therefore require actual construction, minimization and locality checks before any route can be enabled. The independent measurements are retained in ignored `out/reconciled-design/dopc-patch-geometry.json` and `dppc-patch-geometry.json`.

The original installed OpenMM assets remain unchanged. These candidates contain only source material from the [OpenMM 8.6 application data](https://github.com/openmm/openmm/tree/8.6.0/wrappers/python/openmm/app/data), with the two named residues removed on 2026-09-27. The OpenMM project identifies its application layer as MIT-licensed; the source attribution and required MIT permission notice are retained in [the POPC derivative provenance](README-POPC-OpenMM86-63x63.md#attribution-and-rights), which accompanies these assets in this directory. No coordinates from the Lipid21 authors' separate repository are included.
