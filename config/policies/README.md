# Local scientific policy catalogues

`protein-slice1.json` supplies one identified protein chemical-state policy and one bounded structural-assessment policy for the first implementation slice. It deliberately leaves membrane, placement, explicit-preparation, and equilibration catalogues empty. Their absent records cannot authorize later scientific routes.

The catalogue references the exact `amber19/protein.ff19SB.xml` file in the local pinned OpenMM 8.6.0 environment. Its recorded SHA-256 is `086bfdb1c05e7d5cb1d330e6c39fab5056a95e89492cdbd3044c746db13e9d09`; the host and worker check the bytes before use. Rebuilding the Python environment requires checking that digest again. The [OpenMM force-field guide](https://docs.openmm.org/latest/userguide/application/02_running_sims.html#force-fields) identifies ff19SB as the Amber19 protein file. The [Modeller variant reference](https://docs.openmm.org/latest/api-python/generated/openmm.app.modeller.Modeller.html#openmm.app.modeller.Modeller.addHydrogens) identifies the supported ASP/ASH, GLU/GLH, CYS/CYX, HIS/HID/HIE/HIP, and LYS/LYN hydrogen forms and explains why explicit variants are needed to avoid a provider-selected histidine state. The policy's ordinary ASP, GLU, CYS, and LYS selections follow the accepted nominal-pH-7.0 reference; ARG and TYR use their standard ff19SB templates. The [2.5 Å sulfur-proximity rule](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/24/4/498/207792) is used only to surface a possible disulfide for review. It cannot decide that a bond exists.

The structural policy requires measured covalent-bond and consecutive peptide C–N distances on the actual candidate. The installed ff19SB XML declares equilibrium bond lengths from 0.96 Å (O–H) to 2.038 Å (S–S). The 0.75–2.25 Å covalent range is a deliberately broad gross-distortion envelope around those identified parameters, **not** a published bond-type-specific validation threshold. [Engh and Huber](https://doi.org/10.1107/S0108767391001071) establish protein backbone and side-chain target bond geometry from crystallographic data; [REFMAC5's linkage rule](https://journals.iucr.org/d/issues/2004/12/01/ba5073/) uses a 1.728 Å upper C–N distance to recognize a peptide connection. The policy adopts that upper bound and a permissive 1.0 Å lower gross-distortion bound. A one-residue chain has no consecutive peptide pair, so `chainContinuity` may be inapplicable only when its eligible count is zero. The H/C/N/O/S observation radii are the Bondi values reproduced in [published structural analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7079509/); they populate the worker's element-addressing measurement input, not a steric-clash decision.

These bounds are intentionally limited. The worker reports measurements; the C# Protein Preparation owner decides whether the declared coverage, exact correspondence, approved changes, chemical state, parameter route, and actual geometry permit an Assessed Prepared Protein. The catalogue does not implement [MolProbity's pair-specific overlap analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5734394/) or claim comprehensive stereochemical validation. A candidate may need other evidence or a later assessment. A positive first-slice claim also requires exercising representative actual structures through the pinned providers and the official actor route; the policy file and its literature basis alone do not establish that execution evidence.

`maximumSourceAtoms` is a 100,000-atom local intake ceiling to refuse a larger input before expensive interpretation. It is an initial resource bound for this workstation, not a universal molecular support limit. Its adequacy and the chosen geometry envelopes must be checked against representative source and prepared structures before declaring the first slice accepted.

## Slice 2 membrane representations

`protein-membrane-slice2.json` retains the complete first-slice protein records and adds eight precisely identified molecular candidates for the planar bilayer: DLPC, DLPE, DMPC, DPPC, DOPC, POPC, POPE, and cholesterol (`CHL1`). The seven phospholipids are the [OpenMM 8.6.0 bundled membrane-patch types](https://docs.openmm.org/latest/api-python/generated/openmm.app.modeller.Modeller.html#openmm.app.modeller.Modeller.addMembrane); the [OpenMM Amber19 guide](https://docs.openmm.org/latest/userguide/application/02_running_sims.html#force-fields) identifies their Lipid21 parameter stack. The selected `amber19/lipid21.xml` digest is `4ed8dc7485a3df14f55e1d9d12fb2a99813495293ba07c2d910fe0f66185e23a`. Every coordinate asset and the selected XML are separately hashed in the catalogue.

Run `out/python/bin/python scripts/generate-membrane-slice2-assets.py` after the pinned environment is installed to regenerate the seven single-molecule mmCIF files from OpenMM's pinned hydrated patches and the `CHL1` mmCIF from the bundled [RCSB Chemical Component Dictionary `CLR` ideal model](https://www.rcsb.org/ligand/CLR). The source `CLR.cif` was retrieved from `https://files.rcsb.org/ligands/download/CLR.cif` with SHA-256 `0ee512527d26bd087cb19ffe657dada7aae0e35af4595249d126f4b928e6a811`. [RCSB's usage policy](https://www.rcsb.org/pages/usage-policy) makes the CCD data CC0. The author Lipid21 repository's coordinate library was examined as scientific context but is not copied into the product because it has no explicit redistribution license. The generated assets preserve a one-residue atom and bond graph, and the generator requires exact Lipid21 template names, parameterized particle counts, and mmCIF readback.

The older bundled PDB patches wrote five-character acyl atom names beyond the PDB name field; for example, `C210` is read as `0C21`. The generator reverses only this identified `C[23]1x` mapping before writing mmCIF. It takes the first source lipid, whose glycerol `C2` has the natural `2R` handedness; all seven pass a nondegenerate ordered tetrahedral signed-volume check on `O21, C1, C3, HS`. DOPC, POPC, and POPE also pass `9Z` (cis) oleoyl geometry checks. The RCSB `CLR` source explicitly marks eight cholesterol stereocenters. Its heavy carbon names already correspond to OpenMM `CHL1`; the generator maps `O1` to `O3` and hydrogen names by their bonded heavy atom. It preserves all eight source center geometries, exact bonds, and a one-molecule OpenMM parameter match. These `stereoChecks` are included in each catalogue record for the production worker to verify on the actual staged coordinate bytes.

The `areaPerMoleculeAngstromSquared` values for the seven phospholipids are **initial packing footprints** computed as each source patch's observed XY area divided by 64 molecules per leaflet. They are not measured equilibrium areas at 303 K or transferable mixture areas. The volumes for DLPC, DMPC, DPPC, DOPC, POPC and POPE are the pure-bilayer Lipid21 simulation references in [Dickson et al. Table 3](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.jctc.1c01217): respectively 933.23 at 303 K, 1039.32 at 303 K, 1163.50 at 323 K, 1232.84 at 303 K, 1190.32 at 303 K and 1129.94 Å³ at 310 K. DLPE's 907 Å³ is the fully hydrated fluid-phase 35 °C result in [Nagle and Tristram-Nagle Table 6](https://lipid.phys.cmu.edu/bba/bba.pdf), distinct from its 20 °C gel-phase value of 863 Å³. `CHL1`'s 630 Å³ is the approximate partial molecular volume observed in fluid phosphatidylcholine mixtures in [Greenwood et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2695672/); it is composition dependent, so the catalogue gives cholesterol **no transferable packing footprint** (`0.0`). These quantities do not establish a later construction policy or achieved bilayer state.

The membrane policy allows the researcher to state any finite coherent fractions and independently different upper and lower compositions from the eight candidates. Its positive assessment is limited to exact membrane-local molecular representation and combination checks. It makes no blanket conclusion about phase, miscibility, native biological sidedness, cholesterol solubility, leaflet stress, protein compatibility, or a constructed/equilibrated system. Such questions stay with their accepted downstream owners and may prevent a later positive route. The fixed declared baseline remains target 0.15 M NaCl and, if optional equilibration is performed, 303 K.

## Slice 3 placement basis and local PPM installation

`protein-membrane-slice3.json` retains the Slice 2 records and adds one deliberately narrow placement basis for AlkL in symmetric pure DMPC. The full [RCSB 6QWR NMR ensemble](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/6QWR) is stored as `source-assets/6QWR.pdb` with SHA-256 `f4c1503a60321c0cfe513e8211e43e20e2199aa5f71f22429e0e0ae8c97b0779`; the qualified route selects its first model. [RCSB's usage policy](https://www.rcsb.org/pages/usage-policy) identifies deposited coordinates as CC0. This is a sample exact source for the qualified route, not a catalogue of every protein an actor may request. The first NMR model has one AlkL chain and no retained partner. Through the actual protein route it required researcher review of HIS A108 as HID and addition of terminal GLU A211 OXT, then established an assessed 3,205-atom prepared protein. Its prepared bond graph has stable SHA-256 `c3ad823d50c7088490c998e0a094d09e04c11ca4f84563e132525bc57ab37036`. Two generated N-terminal hydrogen coordinates changed slightly across otherwise identical fresh preparations, so the source-level witness binds the exact RCSB bytes and prepared bond graph while leaving `preparedCoordinateSha256` unset; each actual proposal still records and checks its own prepared-coordinate hash and all-atom correspondence.

[Schubeis et al.](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2002598117) determined the AlkL beta-barrel structure by solid-state NMR in DMPC bilayers. [Their supplemental Figure S11](https://www.mpinat.mpg.de/634656/Schubeis_2020_PNAS_Suppl.pdf) independently maps the extracellular loops, periplasmic turns, N-terminal side and transmembrane strands. The witness names source-model residues MET A1 on the lower/periplasmic water side, GLY A38 in extracellular loop L1 on the upper water side, and VAL A73 in a core-contacting strand. These roles were chosen from the published topology map before the PPM result was assessed. The policy requires corresponding measured residues, at least one observed atom within the core and on each water side, and the exact selected DMPC leaflet model; it does not infer support from PPM's energy, broad geometry alone or a favorable picture. The witness's `extracellular-upper; periplasmic-lower` convention relates biological terms to this proposal's physical coordinate labels only when its evidence and measured orientation match.

The separate [PPM 2.0 source distribution](https://cggit.cc.lehigh.edu/biomembhub/ppm2_server_code) was examined at commit `0de704acd10fbf7f4fbafb9bc925e01b06f38ceb` and compiled locally with gfortran 11.4 into ignored `out/ppm2/immers`; its SHA-256 is `d58c7189f27b7e81150ba4f013cdc039aea9cd71c550e86b4796c5df9360f275`. Its required `res.lib` input is kept separately in ignored `out/ppm2/res.lib` with SHA-256 `26ef3b6ee3d237b0c29bcf549b721e33b0b157ccba198b728daa65dc2f817435`. These are local prerequisites, not files redistributed with the product source. The host and worker require their exact hashes before use; the worker stages the library in its request-local PPM directory. `datapar1` and `datasub1` are provider output files. A local build made with other compiler bytes must be identified and requalified rather than silently substituted under this catalogue.

PPM computes an **implicit symmetric DOPC** orientation, not the researcher's DMPC bilayer. The real 6QWR run produced matching ordered heavy atoms, a 19.8 Å plane separation and a rigid rotation/translation of its input heavy coordinates, but omitted input hydrogens. The worker must verify that transform against the provider's heavy output and apply it to every prepared atom before a proposal can preserve the actual chemical construct. The independent AlkL/DMPC witness, exact membrane assessment and Placement Assessment owner supply the support judgment. The source NMR experiment used 20 mM ionic strength at 303 K; the paper's supplemental DMPC simulation used 150 mM KCl. The product's fixed target is 150 mM NaCl, and this bounded placement basis neither reproduces those experimental conditions nor qualifies lipid packing or the native LPS-containing outer membrane.

## Slice 4 native construction and minimization basis

`protein-membrane-slice4.json` carries the earlier policies and adds one versioned preparation policy. Its positive entry scope is the exact first-model `6QWR.pdb` source, one A-chain AlkL copy, no retained partners, the recorded prepared bond graph, accepted chemical-state and structural-assessment policies, residue-variant digest, supported membrane-spanning placement, pure DMPC on both physical sides, and fixed pH 7, 0.15 M NaCl, 303 K conditions. The general class label does not authorize another protein, assembly, state, leaflet composition or condition. The attempt binds the complete policy fingerprint and exact force-field and native-provider assets.

The first bounded construction provider is [OpenMM 8.6 `Modeller.addMembrane`](https://docs.openmm.org/latest/api-python/generated/openmm.app.modeller.Modeller.html#openmm.app.modeller.Modeller.addMembrane), full build `8.6.0.dev-c6173db`. It receives the exact oriented, isolated protein with the source PDB's dummy unit cell cleared, the installed `openmm/app/data/DMPC.pdb` package patch (SHA-256 `19d8513a792ddf6f34edc16028da4410dc6d686945bede006ab1e5a27accb4ba`), 1 nm minimum padding, native Na⁺/Cl⁻ neutralization, and 0.15 M ionic-strength target. The worker verifies the installed package resource before and after the native call. OpenMM tiles the patch, excludes lipids near the protein, performs its own short insertion relaxation, and adds water and ions. That one invocation selects the candidate counts and periodic cell; no earlier dry run, hand-derived population or Packmol recipe sets their values. The product checks the actual returned molecular identities, atom order/bonds/stereochemistry, preserved protein coordinates, cell and protein-image clearance, charge, parameters and contacts. Its proposed and validated values are shown for researcher review before a distinct **Continue minimization** action. This initial provider route does not support the other catalogue lipids, mixtures or independently asymmetric leaflets.

The protein uses the pinned Amber19 ff19SB XML (`086bfdb1c05e7d5cb1d330e6c39fab5056a95e89492cdbd3044c746db13e9d09`), the lipid Lipid21 XML (`4ed8dc7485a3df14f55e1d9d12fb2a99813495293ba07c2d910fe0f66185e23a`), and water/ions the Amber19 TIP3P/Joung-Cheatham XML (`3f4b188dbcb6c02863230eaca231e927fb6bf3307ce947d8a50d0f46f6dd83d9`). The [Amber19 guide](https://docs.openmm.org/latest/userguide/application/02_running_sims.html#force-fields) identifies their intended combination. `scripts/generate-preparation-slice4-assets.py` reproduces one-molecule HOH, NA and CL catalogue reference CIFs with exact hashes `1ca97950cfbf6433587f3164c79c69fab50b5fa86a8f8a564fcc47c5f1464abc`, `e6de3c01860782cf347475b74a56cecbcf8ff00bcd8b81fa66fe1d2649118b89`, and `796c4d55e43f6041833805e839654714feb8b68e1c2c1ccc2f972f996b2e4d1f`. They identify representations but are **not** coordinates consumed by native membrane, solvent or ion construction; the native package patch is a distinct resource.

For the selected first-model 6QWR/DMPC probe, clearing the inherited 1 Å `CRYST1` cell let native OpenMM construct a parameterizable candidate with 112 DMPC molecules per leaflet, 18,524 waters, 58 Na⁺ and 50 Cl⁻ in a 125.818 × 63.306 × 111.478 Å cell. These are observations from that invocation, not required target counts or cell dimensions. The selected protein carries −8 elementary charge. The pinned OpenMM 8.6 ion rule separately supplies eight neutralizing sodium ions and rounds the salt-pair population from the observed water-equivalent count: `floor(0.5 + (18,632 − 8) × 0.15/55.4) = 50`. The achieved water-equivalent estimate is `55.4 × 50 / 18,632 = 0.14867 M`, not a measured bulk concentration. The product applies the same bound version rule to each candidate's actual counts, rather than accepting arbitrary agreement with a fitted tolerance band. The 120,000 atom, 180 Å cell and 900-second bounds are local resource limits, not scientific quality thresholds.

The class policy screens severe *heavy-atom* initial intermolecular overlap at 1.5 Å under periodic minimum-image observation; the all-atom minimum remains a diagnostic because the initial native solvent and ions can have close H-containing or periodic-seam contacts before required minimization. Role-specific observations distinguish protein–lipid contact from water–water, water–ion and ion–ion proximity; only the protein–lipid pair has a construction contact criterion. The policy also requires 24–46 Å upper-minus-lower phosphate-head separation, ±20 Å protein offset from the head midplane, and an observed protein–lipid contact within 6 Å. The native probe had no heavy pair below 1.5 Å; a diagnostic minimization removed all its observed heavy pairs below 2.2 Å and all all-atom pairs below 1.5 Å. These single-case observations support this bounded route but do not prove equilibrated membrane structure or a universal clash cutoff.

The required OpenMM stage uses the selected PME/1 nm cutoff, HBonds constraints and rigid TIP3P water, a 20,000-iteration cap, and the 10 kJ mol⁻¹ nm⁻¹ final physical-force target. For the constrained system the worker observes the Euclidean force projected onto the exact final constraint-tangent space, with per-particle RMS and a checked relative constraint residual; raw State-force RMS is recorded separately because it includes constraint-normal reactions. The diagnostic saved 85,317-particle final State had tangent RMS `8.54095 kJ mol⁻¹ nm⁻¹` and maximum relative constraint error `5.44×10⁻⁸` at OpenMM tolerance `10⁻⁵`. The provider does not expose a reliable private optimizer stop reason or exact iteration count, so those are not inferred from the final force. A completed minimized stage still receives fresh local and protein-geometry observations and separate C# Preparation Assessment.

The catalogue has **no positive completed-stage organization criteria**. Coarse stage geometry may reject a defect, but factual stage completion does not establish an equilibrated, production-ready, or positively qualified membrane. Slice 4 acceptance requires the corrected full product route, researcher review gate, focused and slice tests, and browser comparison with the product-vision views; the diagnostic probe alone is insufficient. Broader proteins, asymmetric mixtures, retained partners, other conditions and optional equilibration require their own applicable evidence and policy.
