# Protein preparation policy catalogue

`protein-slice1.json` supplies one identified protein chemical-state policy and one bounded structural-assessment policy for the first implementation slice. It deliberately leaves membrane, placement, explicit-preparation, and equilibration catalogues empty. Their absent records cannot authorize later scientific routes.

The catalogue references the exact `amber19/protein.ff19SB.xml` file in the local pinned OpenMM 8.6.0 environment. Its recorded SHA-256 is `086bfdb1c05e7d5cb1d330e6c39fab5056a95e89492cdbd3044c746db13e9d09`; the host and worker check the bytes before use. Rebuilding the Python environment requires checking that digest again. The [OpenMM force-field guide](https://docs.openmm.org/latest/userguide/application/02_running_sims.html#force-fields) identifies ff19SB as the Amber19 protein file. The [Modeller variant reference](https://docs.openmm.org/latest/api-python/generated/openmm.app.modeller.Modeller.html#openmm.app.modeller.Modeller.addHydrogens) identifies the supported ASP/ASH, GLU/GLH, CYS/CYX, HIS/HID/HIE/HIP, and LYS/LYN hydrogen forms and explains why explicit variants are needed to avoid a provider-selected histidine state. The policy's ordinary ASP, GLU, CYS, and LYS selections follow the accepted nominal-pH-7.0 reference; ARG and TYR use their standard ff19SB templates. The [2.5 Å sulfur-proximity rule](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/24/4/498/207792) is used only to surface a possible disulfide for review. It cannot decide that a bond exists.

The structural policy requires measured covalent-bond and consecutive peptide C–N distances on the actual candidate. The installed ff19SB XML declares equilibrium bond lengths from 0.96 Å (O–H) to 2.038 Å (S–S). The 0.75–2.25 Å covalent range is a deliberately broad gross-distortion envelope around those identified parameters, **not** a published bond-type-specific validation threshold. [Engh and Huber](https://doi.org/10.1107/S0108767391001071) establish protein backbone and side-chain target bond geometry from crystallographic data; [REFMAC5's linkage rule](https://journals.iucr.org/d/issues/2004/12/01/ba5073/) uses a 1.728 Å upper C–N distance to recognize a peptide connection. The policy adopts that upper bound and a permissive 1.0 Å lower gross-distortion bound. A one-residue chain has no consecutive peptide pair, so `chainContinuity` may be inapplicable only when its eligible count is zero. The H/C/N/O/S observation radii are the Bondi values reproduced in [published structural analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7079509/); they populate the worker's element-addressing measurement input, not a steric-clash decision.

These bounds are intentionally limited. The worker reports measurements; the C# Protein Preparation owner decides whether the declared coverage, exact correspondence, approved changes, chemical state, parameter route, and actual geometry permit an Assessed Prepared Protein. The catalogue does not implement [MolProbity's pair-specific overlap analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5734394/) or claim comprehensive stereochemical validation. A candidate may need other evidence or a later assessment. A positive first-slice claim also requires exercising representative actual structures through the pinned providers and the official actor route; the policy file and its literature basis alone do not establish that execution evidence.

`maximumSourceAtoms` is a 100,000-atom local intake ceiling to refuse a larger input before expensive interpretation. It is an initial resource bound for this workstation, not a universal molecular support limit. Its adequacy and the chosen geometry envelopes must be checked against representative source and prepared structures before declaring the first slice accepted.
